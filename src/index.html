Plug in the Arduino

Dynamis finds the Uno by itself. Nothing to install or select.

  1. Connect a sensor:
    • FX29 load cell: to the R4 WiFi's Qwiic port. Sensor wire → Qwiic wire: red → red, black → black, white → blue, yellow → yellow.
    • Film sensor: its two legs to A0 and GND (either way round, no resistor).
  2. Plug the Uno (R3 or R4 WiFi) into this computer with its USB cable.
  3. Dynamis installs its firmware on the Uno (about 10 s). This replaces the program on the board. Afterwards, upload your own program again from the Arduino IDE.

Calibration

The FX29 is factory-calibrated. Press Zero once with nothing on its button; that's remembered. Press on the center of the button, straight down.

Sensor range

Optional check: put a known weight on the button, wait ~2 s, type it and click Check. If the reading is off, Dynamis corrects it.

g

Correction:

Put a small rigid disc (a coin) on the sensor's round area and a known weight on the disc. Wait ~2 s, type the weight and capture. Use 3–5 weights across your range (e.g. 100 g, 500 g, 1 kg, 2 kg). Use the same disc when measuring.
